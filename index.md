# Privacy Policy – Driver AI Pro

**Last updated: 18 November 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Driver AI Pro is a delivery driver productivity application designed to help drivers track earnings, shifts, delivery jobs, mileage and performance.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## 2. Information stored by the app

Driver AI Pro uses information entered by the user, including:

- Delivery earnings and job details.
- Shift start and finish times.
- Pickup locations and waiting times.
- Mileage and vehicle running costs.
- User preferences and app settings.

This information is stored locally on your device. Driver AI Pro does not upload this information to its own servers or operate an online account system.

## 3. Location information

The app uses your device's GPS location to calculate mileage during delivery shifts.

Location permission may be required while the app is running in the background so that mileage tracking can continue during an active shift.

GPS mileage information is used within the app and is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to Driver AI Pro servers.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings.

## 4. Microphone and voice recognition

Driver AI Pro offers optional voice entry to help users record delivery jobs.

When voice entry is used, the app accesses the microphone and uses the speech recognition service available on your device.

Depending on your device, settings and speech recognition provider, voice data may be processed by Google or another device speech recognition provider.

This processing is subject to that provider's own privacy policy. Driver AI Pro does not operate its own voice recording or transcription servers.

## 5. Data sharing

Driver AI Pro does not sell, rent or share your earnings, job records, pickup information or GPS mileage with third parties.

The app does not upload these records to its own servers.

Third-party services used by your device, including speech recognition services, operate under their own privacy policies.

## 6. Data security and deletion

Your app information is stored locally on your device.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure.

Deleting the app or clearing its data may permanently remove information stored by Driver AI Pro. Consider keeping your own records or backups if you need to retain this information.

## 7. Children's privacy

Driver AI Pro is intended for adults involved in delivery driving and is not designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change.

The latest version will be available at the public privacy policy address provided on the Google Play Store.
